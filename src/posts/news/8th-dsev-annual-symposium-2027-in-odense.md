# SNEV Interconnect Program

The Student Network on Extracellular Vesicles (SNEV) is a global virtual network founded in 2020,dedicated to students and early-career researchers in EV research. SNEV actively contributes to ISEV activities, organizes sessions at annual meetings, fosters collaboration, and promotes early-career engagement within the EV community.

The DSEV board is seeking a PhD student or postdoc to serve as DSEV’s SNEV ambassador. The ambassador will help promote SNEV activities and share relevant opportunities with DSEV’searly-career researchers.

When the DSEV meeting registration opens, there will be a checkbox where you can indicate your interest in this role.

# MOVE European Mobility Fellowship

As in previous years, students and postdocs can apply for a MOVE European Mobility Fellowship to visit a MOVE-registered host lab (see the list here: [MOVE labs)](https://www.ukev.org.uk/move-mobility-ev-europe/)

We will award one travel grant this year, and information on the application procedure and requirements can be found here: [MOVE-requirements](https://dsev.dk/pages/grants/)

The grant will be awarded at the DSEV meeting. 

**Deadline for applying: February 1, 2027.**

# Sandwich Grants

DSEV’s mission is to advance EV research by promoting scientific communication among researchers in Denmark at all career stages. Our annual symposium is an excellent opportunity to connect EV researchers nationwide, and we now also wish to support local EV communities through a new initiative we have termed: "Sandwich Grant". If your local EV network wants to apply for a Sandwich Grant, information can be found here: [Sandwich Grants.](https://dsev.dk/pages/grants/)

**Deadline for applying: February 1, 2027.**
