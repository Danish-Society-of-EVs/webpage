# Registration

## *How to sign up*

Registration and abstract submission will open on December 1, 2026.

## *Registration fee*

Registration for the symposium is linked to a DSEV membership, and the payment of the membership fee of 200 DKK will grant you voting access to the DSEV General Assembly, which takes place during the symposium. This fee also covers breakfast, coffee, and lunch at the symposium. However, if you want to participate in the evening dinner at 8pm, it will cost an additional DKK 200 (note the price increase).

#### \*\*\* New initiative to reduce expense and food waste that could be avoided \*\*\*

In the past years, we have seen a couple of no-shows due to illness etc. at the dinner. The dinner part of the symposium is offered at a lower price than the actual cost, since we believe it promotes networking among DSEV members. This means, DSEV pays more when there are more who join the dinner event. The Board has thus decided to implement "No-show fees" of DKK 500 also in anticipation of minimising the food waste. If unforeseen circumstances prevent you from attending, we kindly ask that you pass your dinner ticket on to another participant so it can be used in your place to avoid the no-show fee.

# Abstract submission

To promote networking across institutions, you are strongly encouraged to submit an abstract, including reuse of abstracts for posters presented at other events. All abstracts will be reviewed and selected for oral or poster presentation (please indicate your preference during abstract submission). Note that registration is required when you submit an abstract. **Abstracts must not exceed 1,500 characters (including spaces).**

# SNEV Interconnect Program

The Student Network onExtracellular Vesicles (SNEV) is a global virtual network founded in 2020,dedicated to students and early-career researchers in EV research. SNEV actively contributes to ISEV activities, organizes sessions at annual meetings, fosters collaboration, and promotes early-career engagement within the EV community.

The DSEV board is seeking a PhD student or postdoc to serve as DSEV’s SNEV ambassador. The ambassador will help promote SNEV activities and share relevant opportunities with DSEV’searly-career researchers.

When the DSEV meeting registration opens, there will be a checkbox where you can indicate your interest in this role.

# Sandwich Grants

DSEV’s mission is to advance EV research by promoting scientific communication among researchers in Denmark at all career stages. Our annual symposium is an excellent opportunity to connect EV researchers nationwide, and we now also wish to support local EV communities through a new initiative we have termed: "Sandwich Grant". If your local EV network wants to apply for a Sandwich Grant, information can be found here: [Sandwich Grants.](https://dsev.dk/pages/grants/)

**Deadline: February 1, 2027.**
