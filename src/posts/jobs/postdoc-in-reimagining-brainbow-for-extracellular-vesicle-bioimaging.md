### Brief job description

Are you passionate about bioimaging and ready to tackle the innovative challenge of engineering rainbow-coloured extracellular vesicles? If you love bioimaging, let's work together to develop a method to capture beautiful images that may also revolutionise our understanding of extracellular vesicles in vivo!

**We have a 2-year postdoc position starting earliest at 1st January 2027.** The project is funded by Villum Foundation through the Villum Experiment grant "*From Genesis to Terminus: Rainbow-Painting of Extracellular Vesicles for Single-Particle Tracking*". Through the project, you will be trained as an expert in the extracellular vesicle field with skills in genetic engineering of extracellular vesicles (including transient/stable transgenesis of zebrafish), live embryo imaging, and super-resolution microscopy of cells.
